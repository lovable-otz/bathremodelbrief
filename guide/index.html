The complete bathroom remodel guide

Reviewed 2026-08-18Every figure cited to a named source

This is the whole bathroom remodel decision in the order you meet it - deciding the scope, working out the layout, getting quotes you can compare, and knowing what the work actually involves. Each stage links to the detailed answers underneath it.

Most people remodel a bathroom once every decade or two. That makes the whole process unfamiliar almost every time, while the people quoting you do it every week. This page is the map: the stages in the order they happen, and what the decision at each stage actually is.

The stages, in order

  1. Decide the scope. A cosmetic refresh, a full gut, or something between - this sets the shape of every quote you will receive. See What It Costs for how scope moves price.
  2. Work out the layout. Whether the plumbing stays where it is, and what that allows or rules out. That is Ideas & Layouts.
  3. Decide who does what. Whether any of it is realistic to do yourself is DIY vs Professional; how to pick and vet a contractor is Choosing a Company.
  4. Get quotes you can compare. Same scope, same finish level, same inclusions, in writing.
  5. Live through the work. What actually happens on site and in what order is How It Works.
  6. Know what you're looking at if something goes wrong later. Common Problems covers the symptoms worth taking seriously.

Two words worth learning before you get quoted

Wet wall is the wall carrying your plumbing supply and drain lines. Keeping fixtures on the existing wet wall is almost always cheaper than moving them, because moving a drain means opening the floor and can mean rerouting the vent stack.

Rough-in is the plumbing and framing work done before any finish surface goes on - the stage where a layout change actually happens, if one is happening. If you learn nothing else before your first contractor meeting, learn to ask whether your layout requires new rough-in.

What this section covers

The pages underneath this one are the general questions about the process: what a full remodel involves, how projects are typically sequenced, and the brands and products people ask about by name. Questions about price sit under What It Costs, and questions about a specific layout or finish sit under Ideas & Layouts.

Common questions

Where should I start if I haven't done anything yet?

With the scope decision in stage 1. Everything downstream - the layout options, the realistic budget, whether DIY makes sense for any part of it - depends on whether this is a refresh or a gut.

How long does a bathroom remodel actually take?

It depends heavily on scope and on whether plumbing moves; the process section covers the typical order of operations and what tends to extend a timeline.

Is this site written by a remodeling contractor?

No. Bath Remodel Brief is an independent information site. We do not design, demolish, plumb, tile, install or warranty anything, and we are not licensed to.
